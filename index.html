How to use

Game view scrolls colour-coded notes toward the hit line (like the drum tutor). Fingering view shows the staff on top and one lane per key below — blocks arrive at circles that light up when that key should be down. Notation view shows the staff; tap a bar to set the loop start, tap another to set the end.

Playing: connect your Aerophone with Connect MIDI (Chrome/Edge on desktop or Android), or use Mic with a real sax (headphones recommended). First time with MIDI, press Calibrate MIDI and play the first note — that fixes any octave/transposition difference.

Practice tools: slow it down, loop a few bars, Wait for me, swing, and Practice all keys to run a lick around the circle of 4ths.

Import: MIDI or MusicXML transcriptions, or type a lick. Pick whether the file's pitches are concert or written for your horn.

Step through: use Prev/Next note (or ← →) to move one note at a time and see the fingering. Tick Advance when I play it to move on whenever you play the shown note.

Shortcuts: Space start/pause · Esc stop · V switch view. Fingerings are standard (written Bb3–C#6); palm-key notes are not charted.
